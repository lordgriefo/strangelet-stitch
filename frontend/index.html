⚡ 1. Workflow Modes: Preset (Auto Spine) vs. Manual (Workbench)

Strangelet Stitch provides a dedicated two-way Mode Switcher located directly above the timeline to toggle seamlessly between algorithmic montage creation and precision non-linear assembly:

⚡ PRESET (AUTO SPINE)

AUTO MONTAGE

Algorithmic Montage Generation: Slices imported footage dynamically based on detected audio transients (Kick, Snare, Hi-hat), downbeats, song energy-arc sections (Intro, Verse, Build, Drop, Chorus, Outro), and Fibonacci frame intervals.

Story Spine Sequencer: Clips with chronological prefix filenames (e.g. 01_intro.mp4, 02_verse.mp4) are preserved and arranged in narrative order across the timeline.

Auto-Loop & Seed Recycling: Playback and generation never halt or freeze when audio extends past imported media. The engine automatically wraps around (mediaList[currentIndex % mediaList.length]).

Dynamic Ken Burns Motion Cycling: Recycled still images automatically vary their Ken Burns motion on each subsequent pass (e.g., Pan Up Zoom In → Slow Pull Out → Diagonal Drift) so the visual rhythm stays constantly fresh.

Exhaustion Modes: Select between Loop & Vary Motion, Ping-Pong, or Hold Last Frame in Panel 2.

✂ MANUAL (WORKBENCH)

HANDS-ON NLE

Unlocked Non-Linear Assembly: Provides an unrestricted manual editing track for freeform dragging, trimming, splitting, and manual clip rearrangement.

Zero Overwrite Guarantee: Manual workbench cuts, splits, and pinned blocks (📌 Pin Block) are locked and protected from being overwritten whenever auto-presets are recalculated.

Precision Block Handles: Drag left edge handles to trim cut in-points; drag right edge handles to expand or contract duration down to individual frames.

Drag-and-Drop Swapping: Drag any video or image card from either bay directly onto a timeline block to replace its media while keeping timecode boundaries intact.

✂️ 3. Timeline Operations & Right-Click Context Menus

Every element on the Strangelet Stitch timeline supports context-aware right-click inspection and manipulation:

🎬 Video Block Right-Click Context Menu

  • ✂ Split Block at Cursor (S or C): Slices the active cut block cleanly into two independent blocks at the cursor timestamp.
  • ⏪ Reverse Clip (REV tag): Reverses playback via FFmpeg -vf reverse or inverts Ken Burns camera sweeps for still images.
  • ⏱️ Speed Multipliers: Switch between 0.5x Half Speed, 1.0x Normal Speed, and 2.0x Double Speed.
  • ✨ Apply Video FX: Localized shader submenu to apply CRT Phosphor, Bleach Bypass, Thermal Solar, Tape Degrade, Sepia Rotogravure, RGB Split Strobe, VHS, or Clear FX.
  • ✂ Cut Block (Ctrl+X): Copies block data to clipboard and deletes block cleanly from timeline.
  • 📋 Copy Block (Ctrl+C): Clones block metadata (source URL/file, duration, in/out offsets, speed, FX, Ken Burns).
  • 📥 Paste Block (Ctrl+V): Inserts clipboard block at playhead or right-click cursor position without corrupting adjacent block offsets.
  • 🗑️ Delete Block (Del): Removes the selected block from the timeline track.

🔊 Audio Waveform Right-Click (Web Audio Slicing)

Non-destructive, beat-aligned memory slicing without altering original audio files on disk:

  • ⏪ Reverse Slice (REV ♫): Flips channel audio buffers backwards in time for the rhythmic slice interval.
  • ✂ Beat Chop / Stutter Repeat: Subdivides the beat slice into 4 rapid rhythmic repeats for glitch/trap fills.
  • ⏱ Half-Speed Tape Stop (0.5x): Stretches sample playback at half rate for authentic vinyl/tape slowdown effects.
  • 🔇 Mute Slice (Drop-out): Drops gain to 0.0 for rhythmic drops and dramatic tension breaks.
  • 🔄 Reset Slice: Removes all slice modifications and restores original pristine audio data.

🗂️ 4. Media Bays & Asset Management

Assets are organized into two dedicated bays with instant reactive timeline synchronization and project hygiene controls:

🎬 Video Clips Bay & 🖼️ Still Images Bay

  • Video Clips Bay: Supports .mp4, .mov, .webm (H.264, VP9, AV1, ProRes). Displays duration, resolution, motion energy scores, and Story Spine badges.
  • Still Images Bay: Supports .png, .jpg, .jpeg, .webp. Driven by the Ken Burns engine with dynamic motion parameters (Zoom In Pan Right, Slow Pull Out, Diagonal Drift).
  • Reactive Timeline Updates: Drag-and-drop or file import immediately updates library counter badges (e.g. STILL IMAGES (X), VIDEO CLIPS (X)) and re-evaluates the timeline, instantly populating tracks if waiting for media.

🧹 Asset Flushing & Session Teardown

  • 🗑 Clear Videos Button: Dedicated button on Video Bay header to flush all video clips from memory without resetting your music track or manual cut markers.
  • 🗑 Clear Stills Button: Dedicated button on Still Bay header to flush all still images without resetting audio or cuts.
  • File → New Session (Ctrl+N): Complete project teardown and clean slate reset. Wipes Web Audio buffers, resets waveform canvas, clears lyrics and subtitle tracks, flushes all cut points and transient markers, and purges all media libraries.
